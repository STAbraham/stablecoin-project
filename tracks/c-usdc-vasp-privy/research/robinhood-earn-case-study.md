# Robinhood Earn case study (Privy blog) — self-custodial DeFi lending in a retail app

**Source:** privy.io/blog/bringing-global-financial-markets-onchain-with-robinhood, captured 2026-09-14 (full-page screenshot in `inbox/processed/privy-blog-robinhood-onchain-markets.png`).

## The facts
- **Robinhood Earn** = "the first decentralized lending product available directly through the Robinhood app." "Eligible users can earn yield on their USDG through a self-custodial wallet embedded" in the app; deposits flow to "decentralized lending markets powered by **Morpho**."
- **Custody:** self-custodial — "users maintain ownership of their assets without managing private keys"; Earn lives "from the same place they already monitor their portfolio."
- **Risk backstop:** "insurance procured through **Lloyd's of London and RELM**."
- **Scope/metrics:** Robinhood Wallet in 120+ countries; Robinhood Chain saw "$80M deposited across the ecosystem" within two weeks of launch. (Deck slide 10 adds: estimated **7% APY** on USDG lending.)
- Privy provides "the wallet infrastructure behind Robinhood Earn."

## Why this is the strongest Track C comparable
1. **Shape-for-shape match:** retail app + embedded self-custodial wallet + stablecoin + Morpho lending yield = precisely Track C's architecture (ours adds the PH VASP ramp leg). A US-listed broker shipping it is strong "this is buildable and marketable" evidence.
2. **The word "eligible"** ("eligible users can earn") implies geographic/eligibility gating exists in exactly the place our C-Q6 question lives — reinforces that Earn availability is a per-market eligibility question to put to Privy, not an assumption.
3. **Insurance precedent (part-answers C-Q7):** a Lloyd's/RELM-style policy over DeFi lending risk is a real, procurable mitigation — belongs in Track C's risk stack and in any counsel conversation about offering DeFi yield to PH retail (C-Q12). Ask Privy whether insurance is part of the Earn offering or was Robinhood-procured.
4. **Note the coin:** Robinhood lends **USDG** (Global Dollar), not USDC — issuer-partner economics may sit behind coin choice. For Track C, coin selection (USDC vs alternatives) may itself carry economics; fold into C-Q13.
